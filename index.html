How to use

Edit in 4 easy steps.

  1. 1
    Buy and download

    Pay ₹399 once. You instantly get one zip with all 399 skills and a step-by-step guide.

  2. 2
    Add the skills to Claude

    Open Claude → Settings → Capabilities → Skills and upload the zip. Using Claude Code? Copy the folder into your skills folder. Takes about 2 minutes.

  3. 3
    Give Claude your clip

    Put your raw video in a folder and tell Claude what you want, in plain words.

    "Add glass widgets and captions to clip.mp4"
  4. 4
    Get your edited reel

    Claude picks the right skill, edits on your own computer and saves the finished video next to the original. Want changes? Just ask.

You need: a Claude plan that supports skills, a Windows or Mac computer, and FFmpeg (free, the guide shows how to install it).

    No. You describe the result you want — “turn these clips into a 30-second beat-synced reel with captions” — and Claude chooses the right skills and runs them. Professionals use it to delegate the repetitive work.

    What do I need to run it?

    A Windows or Mac computer, a paid Claude plan (best with Claude Desktop or Claude Code), and two free tools — FFmpeg and Python. The guide walks you through setup in about five minutes.

    What exactly do I receive?

    An instant download with all 399 skills, category packs, the install guide, a prompt library and a searchable catalogue.

    Can I use it for client work?

    Yes — edit for yourself and for paying clients. Reselling or sharing the skill files is not permitted.

    Does it work on a phone?

    Editing runs on a computer, where Claude can process your media. The finished videos go anywhere.
